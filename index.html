About

Jen Sheerin is a Senior Cloud Solution Architect at Microsoft whose work spans enterprise AI applications, agent architecture, and the Azure foundations that support them. She works
with customer teams to connect business scenarios with technical designs, from product-knowledge assistants to applications that combine enterprise data with conversational experiences.

Her recent work includes retrieval-augmented generation, agent tools, model evaluation, and integration with existing applications and databases. She also works through implementation
details such as private connectivity, application deployment, managed identity, and controlled database access. Her customer engagements combine architecture discussions with technical
working sessions, troubleshooting, and guidance on the dependencies that need to be addressed before deployment can proceed.

Alongside this work, Jen develops presentations, demonstrations, and reusable technical materials that explain architectural choices and implementation patterns. She collaborates with
customer developers, data specialists, and Microsoft account teams, pairing technical guidance with opportunities for customer teams to build their own understanding. Her current focus
includes MCP-based enterprise-data integration and the evaluation and operational practices needed to develop and maintain AI agents.
